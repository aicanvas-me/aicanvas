# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Keep it about the code.

## What is expected

Technical disagreement is welcome and is most of what happens here. Say what you
think is wrong and why, about the work rather than the person. Accept that the
maintainer has the final call on scope, design and what ships.

## What is not accepted

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, sexualised content, publishing
someone's private information, sustained disruption, and spam or promotion
dressed up as an issue.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions and commits in this repository, and any space
where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report through the repository's
[Security tab](https://github.com/aicanvas-me/aicanvas/security/advisories/new),
which is private, or by replying to the maintainer on the thread if it is a
public matter. Reports are read by the maintainer only. The response is one of:
a note, a removed comment, or a block. Serious or repeated behaviour skips
straight to a block.
